NeuroOrbit privacy policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Summary

NeuroOrbit is made by PeteStudioz.ai. It runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information, and it contains no advertising, analytics, or telemetry.

Information stored on your device

NeuroOrbit stores the following in its local app data, and only there:

  • a history of completed training sessions: the date and time, which game was played (Memory, Speed or Focus) and its mode, the score (memory span or time in milliseconds), how many trials were correct, and how long the session took. It is used to show progress charts, the day-by-day table, personal bests, day streaks, today's workout and the coach's suggestions, which are worked out on the device.
  • the selected memory mode and the music and tone settings
  • the best score from the first version of the app, if one was saved

Network use

NeuroOrbit has no account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or in-app network service. It does not connect to any server.

Retention and control

You can remove all of NeuroOrbit's data by uninstalling the app, or by resetting the app in Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > NeuroOrbit > Advanced options.

Children

NeuroOrbit does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not a medical product

NeuroOrbit is a brain-training game. It is not a medical product and does not diagnose, treat, or guarantee a change in intelligence. Its general wellness tips (such as sleep and exercise) are not medical advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. For support or privacy questions, email pete.studioz.ai@gmail.com.
