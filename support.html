NeuroOrbit support

NeuroOrbit is a calm, adaptive working-memory game for Windows, made by PeteStudioz.ai.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11 (x64 or Arm64)
  • Mouse, touch, or keyboard

How to play

  1. Choose Forward (repeat in order) or Reverse (repeat backwards).
  2. Select Begin session, watch the nodes light up, then repeat the pattern.
  3. A session is 12 trials, about three minutes. The pattern grows when you succeed and shrinks after a miss, so it stays at the edge of your ability.

Keyboard

  • 1–7: answer (each node shows its number)
  • Enter or Space: begin, pause, resume, train again
  • Esc: pause
  • M / T: music / node tones on or off

Troubleshooting

The session paused by itself. NeuroOrbit pauses when you switch to another window or minimise it. When you resume, the same pattern is replayed. Interrupting the same pattern twice counts it as missed.

Number keys do nothing. Click anywhere in the NeuroOrbit window so it has keyboard focus, then try again.

No sound. Check that Tones and Music are highlighted at the top of the window, and that NeuroOrbit isn't muted in the Windows volume mixer.

Start over. To clear your history, reset the app in Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > NeuroOrbit > Advanced options.

Accessibility

Each node is a labelled button, so it can be selected with Narrator or other assistive technology. Narrator announces each signal during playback. NeuroOrbit follows the Windows "Animation effects" setting.
