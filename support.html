NeuroOrbit support

NeuroOrbit is a calm, adaptive brain-training program for Windows, made by PeteStudioz.ai. It trains memory, speed and focus with three short games.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11 (x64 or Arm64)
  • Mouse, touch, or keyboard

The games

  • Memory: Orbit Span. Watch the constellation light up, then repeat it in order (Forward) or backwards (Reverse). Your score is your span: the longest pattern you repeated correctly.
  • Speed: Flash. Keep your eyes on the centre. A shape in the centre and a signal at the edge flash together, then are covered. Say which shape it was, then where the signal was. Your score is the shortest display time you can still read.
  • Focus: Steady. Answer which way the centre arrow points and ignore the arrows around it. Your score is the shortest time limit you can still answer within. The summary also shows your distraction cost: how much slower you are when the surrounding arrows point the other way.

Every game adapts to you, so it stays challenging but doable. Today's workout on the home screen plays one session of each, about eight minutes in all.

Progress and coach

Select Progress on the home screen to see your last seven days, a 30-day chart for each skill and a day-by-day table. Scores move with sleep, stress and time of day, so judge the trend over weeks. The coach's suggestions come from your own results and stay on your device.

Keyboard

  • 1–7: answer in Orbit Span; 1–6: choose the signal's place in Flash
  • ← / →: triangle or square in Flash, left or right in Steady
  • Enter or Space: begin, pause, resume, train again, or go to the next game of the workout
  • Esc: pause; with no session running, go back home
  • M / T: music / tones on or off

Troubleshooting

The session paused by itself. NeuroOrbit pauses when you switch to another window or minimise it. When you resume, the trial starts again. Interrupting the same trial twice counts it as missed.

My session wasn't saved. Sessions are saved when they finish. Leaving a game early, or choosing Discard, doesn't save it.

Keys do nothing. Click anywhere in the NeuroOrbit window so it has keyboard focus, then try again.

No sound. Check that Tones and Music are highlighted at the top of a game, and that NeuroOrbit isn't muted in the Windows volume mixer.

Start over. To clear your history, reset the app in Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > NeuroOrbit > Advanced options.

Accessibility

In Orbit Span each node is a labelled button, so it can be selected with Narrator or other assistive technology, and Narrator announces each signal during playback. Flash and Steady are visual timing games. Charts have text descriptions, and every value is also in the day-by-day table. NeuroOrbit follows the Windows "Animation effects" setting.
